Koreans Know Automation

A single-owner production tool for original English-language videos introducing Korean culture through pixel-art animation.

The owner reviews a keyword proposal on mobile. Only an explicitly approved topic can proceed to script preparation, offline English narration, video rendering, and public upload to the owner's Koreans Know YouTube channel. The tool checks channel identity and records upload results to avoid duplicates.

This is a private operational tool, not a public account-registration service. The production repository and Google authorization credentials are not published on this website.
