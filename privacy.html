Privacy and Google data use

Effective October 27, 2026. This private, single-owner tool creates and uploads original Korean-culture videos. There is no public registration, customer database, or advertising service.

Google data accessed and purpose

With the channel owner's authorization, the tool uses YouTube upload and read-only permissions to identify the authorized channel, inspect recent upload metadata for duplicate prevention, upload an approved video, and verify the resulting video ID and public visibility. It does not request Gmail, Drive, contacts, payment, or password access.

Storage and processing

Google OAuth client credentials and refresh tokens are held in encrypted GitHub Actions repository secrets. They are made available to the authorized cloud job that communicates with Google's OAuth and YouTube API services. Channel identity, topic approval references, video IDs, publication status, and timestamps are recorded in the private production repository. Temporary video previews are configured to expire after one day. Workflow logs follow the repository's configured retention period. Publication records remain until the owner removes them.

Sharing and AI

GitHub hosts the private repository, execution jobs, secrets, and temporary artifacts. Google handles authorization and YouTube uploads. Original videos approved for publication and their descriptions are made public on YouTube. Public official cultural source text and draft editorial content may be sent to the owner's free-tier Gemini service for writing and factual review. YouTube authorization credentials, private channel API responses, personal documents, and approval comments are not sent to Gemini.

The tool does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use Google user data to train or improve a generalized AI model. Its use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Owner controls and deletion

The owner can stop cloud workflows, remove the encrypted repository secrets, delete private production records and artifacts, and revoke app access in Google Account connections. Published videos are controlled through the owner's YouTube channel. Revoking access stops future authorized API access; it does not itself remove an already published video.

Contact and changes

For questions about this single-owner tool, contact its owner through the owner's GitHub profile. This disclosure will be updated if data practices change. This static information site adds no app-specific analytics or tracking scripts; hosting services may process ordinary delivery and security logs under their own policies.
