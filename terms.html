Operating terms

Updated October 27, 2026. Koreans Know Automation is a private tool operated by its channel owner, with no public registration or paid customer service.

The tool prepares keyword proposals for the owner's review. Explicit approval of a proposal authorizes production of its associated video and public upload to the Koreans Know channel. A pending proposal, silence, or AI editorial approval does not constitute owner approval. Existing private technical-test videos are not automatically made public.

The tool uses original pixel graphics, synthesized narration, and source-based editorial scripts. The owner remains responsible for reviewing topics and complying with YouTube policies and applicable rights. Technical checks and source review do not guarantee perfect accuracy or availability.

Operation is limited by the configured free-service quotas, authorization status, and daily production limit. Quota exhaustion, uncertain upload results, and authentication failures stop work rather than authorizing payment or duplicate uploads. The tool does not purchase credits or upgrade subscriptions.

YouTube use is also governed by YouTube's Terms of Service. See the privacy and Google data-use disclosure for authorization, storage, and owner controls.
